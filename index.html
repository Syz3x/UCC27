Tournament Rules

1. Group Stage Format

32 teams are divided into 8 groups of 4. Each team plays the others in their group home and away in a round-robin format.

2. Points System

  • Win: 3 Points
  • Draw: 1 Point
  • Loss: 0 Points

3. Tie-Breaking Criteria

If two or more teams are equal on points, the following criteria are applied in order:

  • Head-to-head points
  • Head-to-head goal difference
  • Overall goal difference
  • Overall goals scored
